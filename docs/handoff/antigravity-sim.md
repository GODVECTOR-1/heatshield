  - Use a seeded PRNG, such as mulberry32. Never use `Math.random`.
- **Tasks:** define your own typical construction day in `lib/sim`. You may mirror `TASKS` in `lib/demo/scenario.ts`, but don't import it.
- **Detection** (sensitivity, false alarms, lead time):
  - Run it open-loop on the **planner-off** schedule.
  - Use Tier 0 + 1 only: watch off, and a check-in sampled from the truth model's hydration.

- **Planner metric:** closed-loop with `planShift` on vs off and the compliance rule (Rest 15 min at L ≥ 2, light task at L3, stop at L4).
  - Truth events come from the truth model on the same closed-loop activity.
- **Robustness sweep:** one multiplier m ∈ {0, 0.25, …, 2.0} applied to both the truth model's acclimatisation and fasting effects (`acclimEffect = fastingEffect = m`).
  - The engine and B1 stay frozen; the sweep changes only the truth model.
